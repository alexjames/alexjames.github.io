### Insights
##### Datacenter proliferation
With the promise of AI, we are going to see datacenters and compute turn into its own industry, since it will be the new "oil" of the 21st century.

Given the architectural advantages of the thin client model and fast network connectivity speeds, it's a reasonable guess that most compute will
continue to run on cloud providers. If AI models become a staple for most professions, we are going to need to scale up compute by several orders of magnitude. Even if we can't bet on which model company will dominate the market, we can rest assured of the demand for the infrastructure and energy needed to run these models.
