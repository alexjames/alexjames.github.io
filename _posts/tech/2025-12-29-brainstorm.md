### Simplfying technology for seniors
#### Idea #1
Create a simplified user interface on mobile phones (simple buttons) for common tasks.
 * Pros
   * Straight-forward
 * Challenges
   * Feature parity with features on the phone

#### Idea #2
Completely replace the interface.
Intuition is that seniors care more about the outcome rather than the mechanism e.g. "talk to my grandson", "order groceries", "check the weather".
